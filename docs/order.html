What You Get

Major MIDI ships as a complete Eurorack module, built around the Daisy Patch SM (STM32H750, Cortex-M7):

  • The assembled module, with firmware already installed
  • An SD card
  • A power cable

Nothing to flash and no toolchain to install — put your .mid and .sf2 files on the card and go. The User Manual covers setup and operation, and firmware updates when new releases land.

Don't have .mid or .sf2 files handy? BitMidi has a large library of free MIDI files, and musical-artifacts.com has free SoundFonts.
